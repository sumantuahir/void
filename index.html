Common Inquiries

Where are VOID garments crafted?

All fabrics are sourced and milled in the historical textile hub of Surat, Gujarat, and tailormade in Ahmedabad under strict quality assessments.

What makes Supima Cotton special?

Supima cotton has extra-long fibers that give the yarn twice the strength of regular cotton. It resists pilling, retains dyes perfectly, and grows softer with every single wash.

What is the delivery timeline within India?

We ship all orders via premium couriers. The package usually reaches major metros within 3-4 working days and other regions within 5-7 working days.

The Story

Born in
Ahmedabad.
Made in Surat.

VOID began as a question: why does every Indian clothing brand either look cheap or beg to look Western? The answer was to build something that looked like neither — and like nothing else.

The Beginning

A wardrobe full of
wrong things.

The idea came from a simple frustration. Every professional in India faces the same problem: the market is split between cheap basics that fall apart and imported brands with logos that feel borrowed from someone else's culture. There was nothing in between that was honest, considered, and genuinely Indian in its making.

The Fabric

Surat wasn't a
decision. It was obvious.

India produces some of the finest cotton in the world. Surat — 600 years of textile knowledge, the source city for fabric that ends up in garments sold globally at five times the price. Going there directly was not about cost efficiency. It was about integrity. When the fabric is honest, the garment is honest.

The Name

VOID is a
deliberate absence.

In architecture, the void is the space that defines the form — the empty room that makes the walls meaningful. VOID clothing operates on the same principle. The absence of logos, of graphics, of unnecessary detail — that absence is the design. What remains is the fabric and the cut. That is more than enough.

The Philosophy

Two pieces.
A complete wardrobe.

The constraint is the product. VOID does not expand its range because a season changed. The two pieces in The Edit were chosen because they are the two things every professional actually needs — and almost never finds done properly. We do them properly. That is the entire ambition.
